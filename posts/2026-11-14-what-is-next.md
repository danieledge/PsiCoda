# What is next

*14 November 2026.*

## Safer backups

C: is the netBook's working disk, and it lives in RAM. A flat battery, or loading a new image off the card, and it's gone. PsiBoot already offers to back C: up to the card before switching images, but it keeps just the one copy and overwrites it every time. Back up a C: that has just been wiped and the good copy is gone.

The next version keeps up to three dated backups. Each one is written to a temporary folder first and every file read back and checked before it's given its proper name, and the oldest is only removed once the new one is complete. If C: suddenly looks much smaller than the last backup, it warns you before going ahead. PsiTerm's keys and saved passwords stay off the card. Restoring copies a backup over C: and deletes nothing.

The logic and its tests on the PC are done. The netBook side is in progress.

## A full screen calculator

Calc still draws its Series 5 layout in the middle of the screen. I've designed PsiCalc: a keypad filling the 570 x 480 next to the toolbar, keys big enough for a finger in the theme's colours, everything Calc does including the scientific functions, and a tape of the running sum. Calc stays alongside it. Record has the same layout problem and I haven't got a plan for it yet.
